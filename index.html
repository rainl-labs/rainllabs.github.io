THE FIGHT BEGINS ABOVE THE DARKNESS

Master
the skies.

Take control of a mysterious winged fighter and soar through dangerous environments filled with enemies, obstacles, and powerful bosses. Discover new threats, activate powerful abilities, and unleash devastating attacks when the pressure is at its highest.

Every 10 levels brings a new environment and a boss encounter: Horned Eye, Krakenox, Malrath Doomskull, Arachnoid Prime, Iron Revenant, and Oblivion Nexus. Complete levels, collect stars, and earn coins as you push further into the darkness.

  • 02 6 unique boss battles
  • 03 Multiple environments
  • 04 Combat abilities and power-ups
  • 05 Stars and coin rewards
